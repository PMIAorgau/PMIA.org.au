About Us

Who Are We

PMIA is a Not For Profit Association working towards the welfare of all families, students and migrants, primarily from the Malayalam speaking muslim community in WA, Australia.

Our History

The association was officially formed in mid 2013.

Our Mission

We work towards the upliftment and integration of Malayalee muslims in all aspects. Insha Allah (God willing).

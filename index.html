About Our Association

Who Are We

PMIA is a registered Not-For-Profit Association working dedicatedly towards the welfare of all families, students, and migrants, primarily from the Malayalam-speaking Muslim community in Western Australia.

Community First

Our History

The association was officially formed in mid-2013 by proactive community members seeking to build a supportive, organized platform for Malayalee Muslims in Perth and broader Western Australia.

Established 2013

Our Mission

We work towards the upliftment, spiritual nourishment, and seamless social integration of Malayalee Muslims across all aspects of life, fostering harmony and active civic participation.
